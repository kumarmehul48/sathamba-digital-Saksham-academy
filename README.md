# Sathamba Digital & AI Saksham Academy

Welcome to the official repository and web portal for **Sathamba Digital & AI Saksham Academy**, located in Sathamba Taluka, Aravalli District, Gujarat.

## 🚀 About the Project
Our academy bridges the rural digital divide by transitioning students, job seekers, women, homemakers, and local farmers from basic computer familiarity to high-confidence modern technology and Artificial Intelligence execution.

## 💡 What We Teach & Cover
* **Generative AI & LLMs:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, advanced prompt engineering, automated summarizing, and language localization (Gujarati/English).
* **Web & Application Development:** Modern HTML5, Tailwind CSS, responsive mobile UI layout, and GitHub deployment.
* **Workflow Automation (n8n):** Low-code automation pipelines, connecting applications, and streamlining digital workflows.
* **Office Productivity & Digital Safety:** Google Workspace, spreadsheet calculations, professional correspondence, digital banking, and UPI safety.

## 📁 Repository Structure
