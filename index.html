About SDSA Academy

Bridging the Rural Digital Divide Through Elite Training

Sathamba Digital Saksham Academy (SDSA) was founded with a singular mission: to provide world-class digital education, computer proficiency, and modern artificial intelligence capabilities to Sathamba Taluka and the surrounding Aravalli district.

Our Vision

Creating an AI-fluent, digitally independent rural workforce ready for global opportunities.

Our Mission

Delivering structured 26-week practical training with hands-on projects and certified mentorship.

Certified Tech & AI Mentors

Guided by practical industry experience dedicated to local skills empowerment in Sathamba.

Why Choose SDSA?

  • Structured 26-Week Journey: Comprehensive roadmap covering computers, office productivity, and generative AI.
  • Practical Learning & Workbooks: Interactive workbook system with hands-on tasks and real-time execution.
  • Portfolio Development: Build live web applications and digital projects to showcase to employers.
  • Verified Certificates: Official certification upon successful assessment completion and project review.
Flagship Program

26-Week Digital Skills & AI Master Program

A transformative curriculum designed to take learners from absolute beginners to confident digital practitioners.

Phase 1: Digital Foundations

Computer operating systems, file architecture, professional internet navigation, digital banking security, UPI safety, and professional email etiquette.

Target: Office Assistant, Digital Operator

Phase 2: Office Productivity

Master Google Workspace and Microsoft Office, advanced spreadsheet data management, professional document design, and impactful presentations.

Target: Data Entry & MIS Specialist

Phase 3: AI & Web Creation

Generative AI prompt engineering (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), automated workflows (n8n), web development basics (HTML/Tailwind), and portfolio building.

Target: AI Content Creator, Web Developer
